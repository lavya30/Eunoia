## Creative Direction

- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet engineering confidence — developer-focused, state of the art
- Interpretation: 5 purposeful scenes with smooth crossfades and generous holds. Restraint is the creative choice.
- Angle: Shows the tool actually solving the engineering problem: freehand sketching, text-to-diagram compilation, and multi-user sync working together in harmony.
- Hook: Eunoia brand mark + "Architecture Whiteboard made simple" typing in over warm paper dot-grid
- Outro: Brand mark centered, "Sketch, Diagram, Decide." with mint highlight, "eunoia.dev" hold.
